About ME

Business on one side, systems on the other

I'm an undergraduate reading for a BSc (Hons) in Business Information Systems at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura — a degree that sits deliberately between management and technology. That combination is the whole premise of how I want to work: data on its own is just numbers, and technology on its own is just tooling. What I'm building toward is the part in between, where a dataset gets turned into a decision a business can actually act on.

My current focus is data analysis and business intelligence — querying and cleaning data, building dashboards, and explaining what they mean to someone who doesn't want to look at the raw table. I'm particularly drawn to banking and financial services, where the data is dense and the decisions it supports are consequential.

Outside coursework, I've been teaching myself front-end development by building real small products rather than just following tutorials, and I keep a habit of picking up short courses in adjacent areas — cybersecurity and IoT so far — to keep my sense of the wider technology landscape current.
